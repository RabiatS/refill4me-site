How it works

Three steps, then it runs itself.

Refill Me is tuned to one store at a time: the African, Mexican or Vietnamese grocer you already trust.

Pick your store

Choose a store that has onboarded its shelves. You see their real catalogue and their prices.

Set your refill

Add the things you buy on a rhythm: weekly, every two weeks, monthly. Adjust any time before it runs.

Pick it up

The store confirms, packs and tells you when it is ready. You collect and pay at the counter. Points land in your account.

Questions

The short answers.

Is Refill Me free?

Yes for shoppers. You pay the store's normal prices at the counter when you collect. Stores pay a small subscription and a percentage on app orders, which is what funds your points.

How do I pay?

At pickup, the way you do today. Card payment inside the app is coming after the pilot.

Which stores can I use?

Stores that have onboarded their catalogue. The pilot starts with an African grocery in Houston, with Mexican and Vietnamese stores next. Want yours on the list? Use the form above.

Do you deliver?

Not yet. Pickup is the default, and a store can arrange delivery with you by phone. Delivery partners come after the pilot.

Is there an Android app?

It is coming to Google Play. Until then everything works in your phone's browser at refill4me.com, with the same account.

How do points work?

One point for every whole dollar on a fulfilled order. They show on your home screen. How you spend them is being worked out with the pilot stores.

What happens to my data?

Your orders stay between you and your store. We use aggregate, anonymised demand data to help stores stock better, and never sell personal details. The privacy policy has the full version.

Can I change or cancel a refill?

Any time before it runs: adjust items, move the date, pause it, or delete it. An order that has been placed can be cancelled while the store has not yet started on it.
